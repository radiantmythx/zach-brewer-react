Three ways across one lake

Three art directions for the same shop, drawn from the same reference image and built from the same products and photos. Each one is a complete site: shop, product pages, workshop journal, story, contact, policies and a 404.

Inside any of them, the switcher in the bottom corner jumps to the same page in another direction, so you can compare like with like. Hover or focus a card to wake its live preview.
