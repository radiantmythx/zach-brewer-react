Topo tiles from survey data

Turning a contour map into something you can hold.

  • maps
  • process

Sample entry. Replace with your own.

Survey data comes as a grid of heights. Slice it every few metres and you get contour lines, which is exactly how a printer already thinks: one layer at a time.

The Ridge Line tile uses one printed layer per contour, so the map and the print are the same thing.
