how it's made

Made in layers

  1. 01

    Model

    Every piece starts as a shape on screen: drawn, sculpted, or measured from something real.

  2. 02

    Slice

    Software cuts the model into hundreds of layers, each a fraction of a millimetre thick.

  3. 03

    Print

    The printer lays those layers down one at a time, often through the night.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Supports come off, edges are sanded, and some pieces are primed and painted by hand.
