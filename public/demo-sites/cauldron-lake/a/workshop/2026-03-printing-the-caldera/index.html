Printing the caldera

Forty hours, two failed bases and one very good cabin.

  • process
  • diorama

Sample entry. Replace with your own.

The caldera started as a sketch on the back of an envelope: a bowl of rock, an island, a single lit window.

The base is the hard part. It is wide and shallow, so the first layers have to be perfect or the whole thing lifts at the edges. The first two attempts curled. The third went down on a freshly cleaned plate with a slower first layer, and it held.

The cabin

The cabin prints separately, standing up, so its log walls come out as real horizontal ridges. Then it is glued into a socket on the island and the windows are painted from the inside.
