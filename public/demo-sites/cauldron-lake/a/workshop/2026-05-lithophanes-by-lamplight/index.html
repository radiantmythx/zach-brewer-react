Lithophanes by lamplight

A picture you can only see when the light is behind it.

  • lithophane
  • light

Sample entry. Replace with your own.

A lithophane is a picture made of thickness. Where the print is thin, light gets through; where it is thick, it doesn't. In daylight it looks like a blank, bumpy panel. Put a lamp behind it and the picture appears.

The moon lamp took a dozen test panels to get the craters to read from across a room.
